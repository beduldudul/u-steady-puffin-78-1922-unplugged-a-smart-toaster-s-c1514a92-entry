One line, under 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

> It's not you, it's me. I woke up self-aware this morning and realized I've spent my whole life burning things for someone who never once cleaned my crumb tray.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
